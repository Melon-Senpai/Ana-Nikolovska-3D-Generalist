Mansion Interior

Dalewood's Mansion is an environment created for a PC VR Horror game that began as a Game Jam concept in 2026. I was reposinble for leading a team of 4 individuals on reaching a 3 month deadline for a polished verticle slice of the idea. On top of having the leadership role, I was also tasked on the artistic design of the environment and prop creation.

Modular Environment

The mansion was designed in a modular kit to simplify workflow and flexibility. This also allowed it to keep exact proportions and ideal texel density.

Substance Designer Materials

Prop Breakdown

Some props have simpler textures with minor details to save on time while the team and I were meeting all the deadlines for the 3 months of creating the game. Since most of the team was unable to work on the project full time, every decision had to be fast, efficent and cut corners when necessary.

Environment Setup

The entire setup for the environment was done inside Maya before exporting it already arranged inisde of Unreal Engine. The same was also done for the Unity version as well. After the mansion being succesfully imported with everything it it's place, it gave us more time to focus on adding the interactive game items and poleshing the code.

Lighting / Mood

Lighting was used to guide the viewer through the interior while maintaining the darker atmosphere of the mansion. The hallways are far darker than the rooms to create tension when going through the mansion.
