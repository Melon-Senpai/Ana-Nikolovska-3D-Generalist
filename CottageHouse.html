Reference & Direction

Lonley Cottage is a game ready environment created from an original concept. My goal was to created a comfortable atmosphere, combining realisim with slight stylization to create a dream like effect. I was responsible for the entire environment pipeline, modeling, foliage, texturing and final assembly in Unreal Engine.
I gathered realistic references for the architecture, vegitation and props with stylized environment references for atmosphere.

Blockout & Composition

I established the main proportion of the house during the blockout, the scale and composition. While blocking out I noticed the scene mas missing some elements to it and decided to add a garden.

Asset Creation

I distributed the topology accordingly to each asset's silhouette where needed to maintain their shape, while simpler hard surface assets were kept low poly.

Foliage

I created the foliage using double sided planes and custom texture atlases. Each vegitation group originally used a seperate atlas map. Looking at the project today, I would change this into a shared atlas map to reduce material usage and simplify setup.
Trees were created using TreeIt with a custom leaf texture made in Photoshop. Fruit and vegetable assets were modeled and textured seperatly. In my current workflow, I would place these materials in one shared material to reduce material complexity.
