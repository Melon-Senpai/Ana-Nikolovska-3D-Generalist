Mimic Creatuer Design

The Barrel mimic is an original creature design that began as a 2D concept sketch before I developed it into a 3D creature. The goal was to create a creature variant from my favorite table top game Dungeons and Dragons. The possibilities for the design were endless until I settled down for this depiction at the end. I explored different design choices in Photoshop, focusing heavily on the silhuette and creature elemnts. The design was developed further during the sculpting stage, where I refined the anatomy and forms.
This was one of my earlier passion projects, and revisiting it today I would do a different approach mainly on the materials and UV organization. The original asset uses more material slots than necessary. With my current workflow, I would consolidate the textures and materials to reduce draw calls.
The high poly sculpt was created in ZBrush, with most of the detail focused on the creature's face, mouth and organic transitions between the wooden barrel and fleshy elements.
