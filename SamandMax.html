Sam and Max Office is a fan art environment based on the original point-and-click adventure game. My goal was to recreate the iconic office in a semi-realistic style while retaining the exaggerated, cartoon-inspired forms. I created and textured the assets, produced custom decals and materials, and assembled the final scene in Unreal Engine.

Reference & Direction

Since I had access to the game, I gathered my references through numerous playthroughs, alongside references of real-world objects that were similar to those found in the game. The stickers on the desk and scribbles on Max's table were created by me using Photoshop and Substance Painter. The calendar and sticky notes were also directly recreated from the game. To avoid leaving the picture frames empty, I added original images from different Sam and Max games.

Substance Designer Materials

This project was my first introduction to Substance Designer. I created the floor material and bullet hole texture, which I used as a decal throughout the environment.

Asset Creation

Geometry was distributed based on silhouette and form. Curved or stylized assets have additional edge loops where necessary, while hard-surface pieces were kept more lightweight.
