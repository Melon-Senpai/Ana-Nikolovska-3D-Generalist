Sam and Max Office is a fan art environment based on the original point and click adventure game. My goal was to recreate the iconic office in a semi-realistic style, while retaining the exaggerated, cartoon inspired forms. I created and textured the assets, produced custom decals and materials, and assembled the final scene in Unreal Engine.

Reference & Direction

Since I had access to the game, I gathered my refrences through numerious playthroughs of the game with a mix of real world objects that were similar enough to the game. The stickers on the desk and scribbles on Max's table are all created by me using Photoshop and Substance Painter. The callendar and sticky notes are also directly coppied from the game. To not leave the frames empty, I added original images of different Sam and Max games in their place.

Substance Designer Materials

This project was my first introduction to Substance Designer. I created the floor material and bullet hole texture, which I used as a decal throughout the environment.

Asset Creation

Geometry was distributed based on silhouette and form. Curved or stylized assets have additional edge loops where it was necessary, while hard surface pieces were kept more light weight.
